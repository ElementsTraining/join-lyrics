I was standing by the well
When a Jewish man
Saw the thirst within me
Without hearing me, he knew
And offered me water that flows endlessly

Give me of your water; I am thirsty
I will no longer search in other fountains

I will not return here anymore, to quench the thirst I had before
Ever since I heard the sound of your voice

You are the one whom my soul waited for
The fountain of life that rescued my soul

You are Heaven's gift, the Messiah
You are my Saviour
